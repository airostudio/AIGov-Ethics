About This Academy

Professional development for responsible AI governance

Our Mission

The AI Governance & Ethics Academy exists to equip government IT professionals and line managers with the knowledge, frameworks, and practical skills needed to deploy AI systems responsibly. As AI becomes increasingly integrated into public services, the need for informed governance has never been more critical.

Who This Is For

IT Professionals

Technical staff involved in AI system development, deployment, and maintenance who need to understand governance requirements and implement appropriate safeguards.

Line Managers

Decision-makers who oversee AI initiatives and need to ensure compliance, manage risks, and build accountability structures.

Policy Advisors

Those shaping organizational AI policies who need comprehensive understanding of the regulatory landscape and best practices.

Our Approach

  • Evidence-Based: All content is grounded in peer-reviewed research, official regulatory documents, and documented case studies.
  • Practical: Every concept is connected to actionable frameworks and real-world implementation guidance.
  • Current: Content is regularly updated to reflect the rapidly evolving regulatory landscape.
  • Accessible: Complex topics are presented in clear, understandable language without sacrificing accuracy.
